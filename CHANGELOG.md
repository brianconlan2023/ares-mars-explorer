# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-11-18
First release. Real-time Mars globe, 17 sites, Phobos and Deimos, a drivable rover in Jezero, the New Jezero base vision with the Manifest timeline, sounds, and Learn tabs.

## 1.0.1 — 2026-11-19
- Softer dusk: day fades into night through a short twilight band instead of a hard cut.
- The night side now shows the ground faintly by starlight, in deep rust-brown, instead of flat black.
- Crater relief eases off near the terminator, so low sunlight no longer turns the surface into a dark, embossed "charcoal" look.
